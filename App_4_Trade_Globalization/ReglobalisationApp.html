📄 Abstract
This paper develops a unified model of re-globalisation in which geopolitical risk endogenously reshapes comparative advantage across five major trading blocs. We introduce the Geopolitical Comparative Advantage (GCA) operator and embed it in a strategic dynamic game solved via Bellman's recursive approach. Results reveal a Kantian cooperative equilibrium that Pareto-dominates the Nash trade-war outcome by approximately 1.8% of world consumption.
