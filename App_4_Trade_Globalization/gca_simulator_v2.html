Section 01

Introduction & Framework

Interactive simulations for the GCA framework, which extends classical HOS and Krugman trade theory with national security externalities.

Classical HOS Theory

Comparative advantage determined by factor endowments. Countries export goods intensive in their abundant factor. Four core theorems: Heckscher–Ohlin, Stolper–Samuelson, Rybczynski, Factor Price Equalisation.

Krugman (1980) — New Trade Theory

Intra-industry trade driven by economies of scale and product differentiation under monopolistic competition. Trade generates variety gains even without comparative advantage. Import protection can promote exports.

Geostrategic Concern

Weaponised interdependence (Farrell & Newman 2019). Supply-chain fragility exposed by COVID-19 and Ukraine war. Baldwin's 'second unbundling' severs firm–worker interests. GATT Article XXI inadequate for modern security needs.

GCA Framework (This Paper)

Sovereign Security Index (SSI) augments welfare. Modified Rybczynski, Stolper–Samuelson, and gains-from-trade theorems. Optimal defence tariff, subsidy, and offset instruments. Security premium can reverse classical trade patterns.

Core Equations
Definition — Sovereign Security Index (SSI)
Good k is strategic if domestic production below threshold q̄ₖ creates a security externality.
Σᵢₖ = 1 − αₖ(1 − xᵢₖ/q̄ₖ)^βₖ for xᵢₖ < q̄ₖ Σᵢₖ = 1 for xᵢₖ ≥ q̄ₖ
αₖ ∈ (0,1] = strategic severity  ·  βₖ > 0 = convexity of penalty
Theorem 1 — Geostrategic Comparative Advantage
Country H has GCA in good 1 (non-strategic) if and only if:
a₁ᴴ/a₂ᴴ < (a₁ᶠ/a₂ᶠ) · (1 + μ₂ᶠ) / (1 + μ₂ᴴ)
μ₂ᴴ = λ₂ᴴ · α₂ · β₂ / q̄₂ is the security premium. A large μ₂ᴴ can reverse classical trade patterns.
Theorem 4 — Optimal Defence Trade Policy
The socially optimal DSTP instruments are:
s*ₖ = λᵢₖ · αₖβₖ · (q̄ₖ − xᵢₖ)^(βₖ−1) · q̄ₖ^(−βₖ) τ*ₖ = s*ₖ + t^ToT δ*ₖ = s*ₖ · γᵢₖ
All three converge to zero as xᵢₖ → q̄ₖ — built-in sunset provision.
Proposition 1 — Variety–Security Trade-off
Optimal domestic variety count in the strategic sector:
n*₂ᵢ = (Lᵢ / f₂σ) · (1 + μ₂ᵢ/σ)
Security premium μ₂ᵢ justifies a variety-expansion subsidy beyond infant-industry arguments.
