DSGE Model

Formal structure — Euler equations and steady-state derivations

Lifetime Utility

𝒰 = E₀ Σ βᵗ [ (Cₜ^(1−σ)−1)/(1−σ) − χ Lₜ^(1+φ)/(1+φ) − κ 𝓔ₜ ]

β = discount factor · σ = risk aversion · φ⁻¹ = Frisch elasticity · κ = pollution disutility

Standard Euler Equation

Cₜ^(−σ) = β E[( 1+rₜ₊₁ )(Pₜ/Pₜ₊₁) Cₜ₊₁^(−σ)]
Ĉₜ = Eₜ Ĉₜ₊₁ − (1/σ)(r̂ₜ₊₁ − Eₜ π̂ₜ₊₁) + εₜᶜ

Armington Aggregator with Home-Bias Wedge

Cᵍₜ = [ λ_HB^(1/θ) (c^(g,d))^((θ−1)/θ) + (1−λ_HB)^(1/θ) (c^(g,m))^((θ−1)/θ) ]^(θ/(θ−1))
Import ratio: c^(g,m)/c^(g,d) = [(1−λ_HB)/λ_HB] · (q^g)^(−θ)

Green-Sector Euler Equation (Log-Linearised)

Ĉᵍₜ = Eₜ Ĉᵍₜ₊₁ − (ρ/σ)(r̂ₜ₊₁ − Eₜ π̂ᵍₜ₊₁) − ((ρ−1)/σ) Eₜ[Ĉₜ₊₁−Ĉₜ] + Ω_λ λ̂ₜ + εᵍₜ

Ω_λ = θ·(1−λ̄)/λ̄ · s̄ᵐ_g · σ/ρ > 0 (coefficient on home-bias in green Euler equation)
